# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

Helix Core is a scientific tool, and its community is open to anyone regardless of
experience level, background, nationality, disability, gender identity and expression,
sexual orientation, religion, or academic standing. Everyone taking part in this project —
issues, pull requests, discussions, and any other project space — is expected to help keep
it a place where people can ask questions and disagree about science without being
harassed.

## Expected behavior

- Be direct about technical problems and considerate about people.
- Critique the code, the method, or the result — not the person who wrote it.
- Accept correction gracefully, and correct others without contempt. Being wrong about a
  method is a normal part of research.
- Assume the other person is acting in good faith until there is clear evidence otherwise.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, intimidation, or sustained disruption of discussion.
- Insults, demeaning remarks, or personal attacks, including about someone's competence,
  institution, or command of English.
- Sexualized language or imagery, and unwelcome sexual attention.
- Publishing others' private information without explicit permission.
- Deliberately submitting misleading scientific claims, fabricated results, or code
  intended to cause harm.

## Scope

This applies in all project spaces — the repository, issues, pull requests, and any public
space where someone is representing the project.

## Reporting

Report unacceptable behavior to **AAlhfnawy@nu.edu.eg**. Reports are handled confidentially
and reviewed as promptly as the maintainer's schedule allows. Reporting in good faith will
never count against you.

## Enforcement

The maintainer may take any action judged appropriate to the severity of the incident,
including a private warning, editing or removing offending contributions, or temporarily or
permanently blocking a participant from the project spaces. Where the response is not a
simple removal of content, the reason will be communicated to the person concerned.

This Code of Conduct is adapted in spirit from the
[Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org), version 2.1.
